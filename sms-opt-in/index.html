SMS Opt-In & Verbal Consent

How users consent to receive customer-service and missed-call follow-up text messages from Rui Song.

Program purpose

This SMS program is used for customer-service conversations and missed-call follow-up. It is not used for unsolicited bulk marketing or promotional blasts.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help.

How users opt in

1
The user calls the Rui Song business phone number.
2
Before the call is forwarded, an automated voice prompt explains the SMS program and asks the caller whether they agree to receive customer-service text messages, including missed-call follow-up.
3
The caller provides affirmative consent by pressing 1.
4
Only callers who provide consent are eligible to receive an automated missed-call follow-up text. If the caller replies, the conversation may continue with an AI-assisted customer-service agent.

Verbal consent script

The automated prompt used before collecting consent is:

“Would you like to receive customer-service text messages from Rui Song, including a follow-up text if we miss your call? Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Press 1 to agree.”

Consent is not a condition of purchase. Callers who do not press 1 are not enrolled in SMS follow-up through this verbal-consent flow.

Example messages

Missed-call follow-up

“Rui Song: Sorry we missed your call. Reply to this message and our AI-assisted customer-service agent can help. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.”

Customer-service reply

“Rui Song: Thanks for your message. How can we help you today? Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.”

Opt-out and help

Users may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Users may reply HELP for assistance. Opt-out requests are honored for future messages from this program.
