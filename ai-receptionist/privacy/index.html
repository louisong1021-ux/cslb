Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Rui Song collects, uses, and protects information in connection with customer-service calls, text messages, and AI-assisted messaging.

SMS privacy commitment: Mobile information, including phone numbers and SMS opt-in/consent information, will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Information we may collect

  • Phone number and basic contact information you provide.
  • Text-message content and customer-service questions.
  • Call and message metadata such as timestamps and delivery status.
  • SMS consent, opt-in, opt-out, and HELP request records.
  • Information you voluntarily provide during a customer-service conversation.

How we use information

We use information to provide and improve customer service, respond to inquiries, send permitted missed-call follow-up messages, maintain consent and opt-out records, troubleshoot the service, and protect against misuse or fraud.

Some customer-service replies may be generated or assisted by artificial intelligence. Information from the conversation may be processed by service providers solely as needed to operate the messaging service.

Service providers

We may use vendors that provide communications, hosting, security, analytics, or AI-processing services. These providers may process information on our behalf only as necessary to provide their services to us and subject to their applicable contractual and privacy obligations.

We do not share mobile phone numbers or SMS consent information with third parties for third-party marketing or promotional use.

SMS choices

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out of SMS messages from this program. Reply HELP for help.

Opting out of SMS does not necessarily delete records we are required or permitted to retain for compliance, security, fraud prevention, or recordkeeping purposes.

Data retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including customer service, compliance, security, and dispute resolution. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children and emergency use

This messaging service is intended for general business customer-service use and is not designed for children under 13. It is also not an emergency service. For emergencies, contact the appropriate emergency services directly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above indicates the latest version.
